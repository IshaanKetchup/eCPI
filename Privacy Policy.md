# Privacy Policy

**eCPI**  
Effective Date: 2nd February 2025

**eCPI** is a simple Collective Pitch Indicator (calculator) app that prioritizes your privacy. This app does **not** collect, store, use, transmit, or share any user data—**period**.

## Key Points

- **No Data Collection**  
  The app does not collect or process any personal data or sensitive information.

- **No Data Storage**  
  We do not store any data locally or on external servers.

- **No Network Activity**  
  The app works entirely offline. It does not connect to the internet, send data, or request permissions.

- **No Ads, No Tracking**  
  The app contains no advertisements, tracking scripts, or analytics.

- **No Permissions**  
  The app does not request or require any special permissions.
